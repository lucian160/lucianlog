How it works

From your application to your dashboard.

Start monitoring your application in a few simple steps.

01

Create a project

Create a project in Lucian Logs and get the credentials needed to connect your application.

02

Send your logs

Connect your application to the Lucian Logs API and start sending useful events from your code.

03

Monitor your application

Open your dashboard to inspect activity, identify errors, and understand what is happening in your app.

04

Fix issues faster

Use the information from your logs to investigate problems and get your application back on track.
